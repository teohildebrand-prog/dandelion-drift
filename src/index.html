Run, jump, hang off ledges, glide on a dandelion seed and grow your own updrafts on the way to the Old Oak crown.

The Wasp Chimney, as built

The GDD's 3-minute sample challenge, laid out at its real heights. The camera pans up the chimney the first time you reach the mossy ledge.

  1. 0:15–0:35
    First lift

    Jump, open the seed over the steam vent on the right and ride it 12 m to the east ledge. Twenty pollen motes spiral up the steam.

  2. 0:35–0:55
    Crosswind timing

    Gusts blow out of the crack in the west wall. Watch the grass bend, wait for the gap, then jump and glide 11 m across to the west ledge.

  3. 0:55–1:20
    First plant

    Take a pod from the seed head, plant a Fan Fern on the damp patch and ride it 15 m to the ledge above.

  4. 1:20–1:50
    Wasp pass

    The swarm loops clockwise around the nest. Glide east to the warm rock thermal and climb while they swing low and face left, or pulse them with Q.

  5. 1:50–2:10
    Turbulence squeeze

    From the high east ledge, tuck dive west through the pocket in the neck. Gliding into it closes your seed. Flare to land on the top shelf.

  6. 2:10–3:00
    The puzzle

    One pod, two patches. A Fan Fern on the ash patch reaches the exit ledge. A Sunpod there climbs 30 m to the hidden alcove and its golden seed, but only while the cloud shadow is away.
